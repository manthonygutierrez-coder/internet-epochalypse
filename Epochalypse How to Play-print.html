OPEN ANYWHEREany browser · no app

1

Open the file

Double-click the HTML file — it opens in any web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge). Nothing to install. Click once anywhere to turn on the sound.

2

Travel through time

Drag to orbit the world. Scroll or swipe to fly forward and back through six eras — the whole interface reskins as you arrive in each one.

3

Find the glowing tags

Each era holds a ▶ green tag floating on a device or screen. That's a real, working artifact from that era — click it to open it.

4

Play, then come back

Dial a modem, customize a profile, refresh a feed, generate an image — and play Capsumon. Press ESC or ✕ to return to the timeline.

The Quest

Wild capsumon hide in the tall grass. Weaken one with A, then throw a capsule — the weaker it is, the better it sticks.

  • Fill the DEX — catch all 6 species across the meadow, forest & cave.
  • Walk into a building to meet trainers and their capsumon.
  • Rest free at the BUD STOP to fully heal your team.
  • Fainting just sends you home — you never lose progress.
